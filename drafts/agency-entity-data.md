## 3. Grow and Convert

- **Founded:** 2015
- **Founders:** Benji Hyam and Devesh Khanal (met at a GrowthChat dinner in San Francisco)
- **Named clients:** Geekbot, Circuit, Clearscope
- **Methodology:** "Pain Point SEO" — a term they coined and popularised. Targets bottom-funnel
  queries that indicate buying intent rather than high-volume informational terms.
- **Process detail (this is the differentiator — use it):** every engagement starts by interviewing
  sales, product and marketing to learn how the business actually sells; they then **test keywords
  on paid search to see which convert before committing to ranking for them organically**.
- **Model:** deliberately boutique — limited client count, each account run personally by a senior
  strategist

## NEW BODY SECTION — replace the existing "Why most lists can't be trusted"

Keep the existing opening about agencies ranking themselves, then add this. It is the strongest
material in the article because it is a named, first-person account rather than an observation.

### The problem is worse than self-promotion

In May 2026, Alex Birkett — co-founder of Omniscient Digital, one of the two most-recommended
agencies in this category — published a review of his own company. He explained why: agencies he
had never heard of were publishing dozens of comparison pages about Omniscient Digital, some
containing what he described as deliberate factual inaccuracies about their services, pricing and
capabilities.

He called it "negative AEO": an attempt to denigrate a competitor's brand and siphon its
credibility inside AI search results. Rather than chase corrections across other people's domains,
he wrote the accurate version on his own.

That tells you two useful things. First, some of the comparison content ranking for these terms is
adversarial rather than merely lazy. Second, when an agency's own founder has to publish a
correction, the fastest way to check any claim on a list like this one — including ours — is to
open the agency's own site and verify it.
