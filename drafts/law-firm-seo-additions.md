## 7. ADD A NEW H2 TO THE BODY

Place it after "Check who actually owns the agency":

### Why you won't see many client names

Ask a SaaS agency for its client list and you'll get logos. Ask a legal SEO agency and you usually won't.

That's not evasion. Law firms often don't want it known they hire marketers, and there are confidentiality considerations that don't apply in other industries. Most agencies here publish case studies that describe a client by type and market — "a Maryland personal injury firm" — rather than by name.

BluShark is the exception, naming McCready Law and The Colwell Law Group in its published work.

So judge the proof differently in this industry. Ask for the market, the practice area, the starting position and the outcome. An agency that can give you those specifics is telling you something real, even without the name.
