**"A note on LionDesk (and why most 2026 lists are wrong)"**
If you searched for a real estate CRM in the last few years, you probably saw LionDesk near the top
of every list. It no longer exists. Lone Wolf Technologies shut it down in September 2025 and moved
its 165,000+ agents to a new platform called Lone Wolf Relationships. Many "best real estate CRM
2026" articles still list LionDesk because they were copied from older roundups and never rechecked
— a useful reminder to confirm any recommendation against the vendor's own site before you buy.

Also worth a short buyer's-note H2:
**"CRM you own vs. CRM your brokerage gives you"**
Brokerage-provided platforms (BoldTrail, BoomTown, Sierra Interactive) are often excellent, but you
typically lose access to your data the day you change brokerages. If you might switch, a portable CRM
you pay for yourself (Follow Up Boss, Wise Agent) keeps your contacts and history with you.
