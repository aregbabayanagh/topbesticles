## intro

Almost every "best SaaS SEO agency" list is written by an SEO agency. Most of them put themselves at number one. We don't sell SEO, so this list is ordered by what each agency is actually good at.

---

## ITEM DESCRIPTIONS

### 1. SimpleTiger — Best overall for B2B SaaS

SimpleTiger has done SaaS SEO since 2006. That makes it the longest-running SaaS-only search agency here.

It's one of only two agencies on this list that competitors name without being paid to. That's the closest thing to an independent signal this market offers. They prioritise keywords by revenue impact rather than search volume, and they do the technical work themselves instead of handing over a document. Best fit: product-led growth companies, where free trials matter more than form fills.

---

### 2. Omniscient Digital — Best for growth-stage content

Omniscient Digital was founded by operators from HubSpot and Shopify. It shows in how they work.

They're strategy-led and editorially demanding. The focus is building topical authority over time, not publishing volume. Clients include Jasper, Hotjar and SpotDraft. If your content is technically fine but forgettable, this is the agency that fixes that. They only take B2B software — no ecommerce, no consumer apps.

---

### 3. Embarque — Best for early-stage and lean budgets

Embarque is built for the stage most agencies avoid: companies still working out whether organic search is worth it.

Pricing is productized and public — flat monthly fee from $1,800, three-month minimum. That's rare in this market. Clients include VEED and Riverside. The trade-off is honest: at this price you get consistent content and keyword coverage, not custom technical SEO or a full enterprise programme.

---

### 4. Grow and Convert — Best when traffic isn't converting

There's a common failure in SaaS SEO. Rankings improve, traffic climbs, signups don't move.

Grow and Convert built their reputation on that exact problem. They coined "Pain Point SEO" — targeting bottom-funnel queries that signal buying intent, not high-volume informational terms. Every engagement starts by interviewing sales and product to learn how the business actually sells. Then they test keywords on paid search to see which convert before committing to rank for them organically.

---

### 5. Powered by Search — Best for enterprise demand gen

Powered by Search treats SEO as one part of demand generation, not a standalone channel.

They map content to buying committees and customer lifetime value. That framing only makes sense at scale. If you sell a $29/month product, it's overkill. If you have a six-month sales cycle and five people on the buying committee, it's what you need. Strong in cybersecurity and enterprise SaaS. Clients include Basecamp, VMware and ClickFunnels.

---

### 6. Animalz — Best for category-defining content

You hire Animalz to own a conversation, not to fill a funnel.

Their model is editorial-first and long-horizon: deeply researched, opinionated content that makes your company the reference point in its category. Clients include Google, Amazon, Intercom and Slack. One reviewer put it well — this isn't the agency you hire when you need trial signups in 90 days. Believe that. It's the most useful thing to know about them.

---

### 7. Skale — Best for scaling organic signups

Skale measures itself on commercial metrics: organic signups, qualified pipeline, revenue. Not rankings or sessions.

They build a forecast model tying search visibility to leads and recurring revenue before any content ships. You get a dedicated six-person team that works inside your marketing department. Consulting starts at $5,000/month, management at $10,000. That $10k floor puts them above several specialists here, with no lower tier.

---

### 8. Directive — Best for paid plus organic together

Directive isn't a SaaS SEO specialist. It's a performance agency with deep SaaS experience.

They cover content, conversion optimisation and paid media alongside organic. That breadth is both the reason to hire them and the reason not to. If you want one partner accountable for the whole acquisition picture, that's real value. If you want the deepest SaaS SEO expertise available, the specialists above go further. Clients include Allstate, Cisco and Sumo Logic.

---

### 9. Siege Media — Best for links at scale

Siege Media's reputation is built on content that earns links, rather than links you buy.

Most agencies split editorial production and digital PR into two line items. Siege does both. If domain authority is what's holding you back in a competitive category, that's the relevant skill. Clients include Airbnb, Zapier, Asana and Zillow. Founder Ross Hudgens has a book on Generative Engine Optimization coming from Wiley in late 2026 — a sign they're taking AI search seriously.
